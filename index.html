What is LimiMake?

LimiMake is an area of LimiPlake ↖︎
where creators come
together to learn to build
awesome things. From simple
animated stories in ScratchJr to
advanced forms using HTML, CSS,
and JavaScript, LimiMake inspires
children and adults to be creative
and turn their ideas into reality.

What You'll Learn

LimiMake has five levels:

  1. ScratchJr
  2. Scratch
  3. Logo
  4. Python
  5. Web Development with
    HTML, CSS, and
    JavaScript

Additional Further Courses↖︎ are also available.
